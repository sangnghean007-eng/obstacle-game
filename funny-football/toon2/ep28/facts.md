# Ep28 facts — Sweden 2-3 Portugal, 19 Nov 2013 (2014 World Cup play-off, 2nd leg, Friends Arena, Solna)
- 1st leg (Lisbon, 15 Nov 2013): Portugal 1-0 Sweden, Ronaldo goal.
- 2nd leg: Ronaldo 50' (1-0) · Ibrahimović 68' header from a Kallström corner (1-1) · Ibrahimović 72' free kick (2-1) · Ronaldo 77' (2-2) · Ronaldo 79' after a João Moutinho pass (2-3, hat-trick).
- At 2-1 the aggregate was 2-2 but Portugal led on away goals: Sweden needed one more goal.
- Portugal through 4-2 on aggregate to Brazil 2014.
- Zlatan after the match (widely reported): "One thing is for sure, a World Cup without me is nothing to watch." He never played at a World Cup again.
Sources: UEFA (uefa.com/european-qualifiers/news/newsid=2024659.html), Sports Mole match page + live commentary, SI (si.com 2013/11/19), Herald/AP, VnExpress, Dân Trí, Thanh Niên.
