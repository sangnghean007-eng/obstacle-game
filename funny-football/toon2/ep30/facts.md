# Ep30 facts — "Two legends, two goodbyes" (re-checked 21 Nov 2026 after the owner flagged it)
MESSI
- Last Argentina game: 6 Oct 2026, Argentina 3-0 Benin, Estadio Monumental ("the last tango"). Game paused at 10' for a standing ovation.
- Penalty = 126th and last international goal; 2 assists. 208 caps. Players from the 1978/1986/2022 squads joined the tribute.
RONALDO
- Left on the bench for the full 90 minutes by coach Jorge Jesus in a Nations League game vs Croatia.
- Walked out of the Portugal camp in Denmark; said Jesus broke a promise to him twice.
- 5 Nov 2026: announced his international retirement in a long video on social media ("I leave with my head held high"). 234 caps, 146 goals (all-time top scorer), Euro 2016.
- Portugal's first game without him: lost 1-4 to Denmark (Braga); then won 3-2 in Wales.
