# Draft: "THE DREAM DUO 🐐🐐 — Ronaldo × Messi on the same team" (Ep23, ~22 s) — for owner sign-off before any video
Owner idea (Nov 19): build the viral amateur-skills montage (study in BIG_CHANNELS.md) into Ronaldo + Messi combination plays.

## 1. Research / facts
- Ronaldo and Messi have **never played on the same team** (club or country); they only met as opponents (Clásicos, UCL, friendlies). Common knowledge, confirmed by every career record (Wikipedia career pages; Transfermarkt).
- Ballon d'Ors: **Messi 8, Ronaldo 5 = 13** (see ep15/facts.md; Ballon d'Or official list).
- On screen only: "They never played together… what if they did?" and "13 Ballon d'Ors on one pitch". Everything else = cartoon parody.
- What we take from the reference reel: payoff in the first 1–2 s, a new payoff every 2–3 s, no story setup, real-feeling pitches (dirt, artificial grass, futsal court), designed for SHARES. We do NOT use or copy any of its footage.

## 2. Format
A "phone-filmed" combo montage: each clip 2.5 s, shot like an amateur video (slight handheld shake, low angle from the touchline, grainy), a different pitch each time, each clip ends on an impossible combo between the two. A running counter "COMBO 1/6…". No speech bubbles; one caption at the start, one question at the end.
