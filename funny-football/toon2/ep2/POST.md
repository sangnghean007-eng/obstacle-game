# Episode 2: "The GOAT Dictionary" (9:16, 20 s, English)

## Fact check (done before production)
- Oct 28, 2020: Juventus 0-2 Barcelona (Champions League, Turin). Ronaldo missed it while isolating with COVID-19. Barça posted a Messi photo about seeing "the GOAT" play there; Juventus replied that Barça had looked in the "wrong dictionary" and would bring the right one to Camp Nou. Sources: [Goal](https://www.goal.com/en/news/were-glad-you-got-to-see-the-goat---barca-trolls-ronaldo-and-juventus-after-champions-league-win/1yrmty5p0k3l1nsebfvd920hv), [Football Italia](https://football-italia.net/juventus-troll-barcelona-over-goat-tweet/), [WION](https://www.wionews.com/sports/wrong-dictionary-juventus-cheeky-response-to-barcelona-triggers-battle-of-goats-338783).
- Dec 8, 2020: Barcelona 0-3 Juventus at Camp Nou. Ronaldo scored 2 penalties, McKennie scored the other goal. Juventus then posted "We kept our word: we brought it!" Sources: [SI](https://www.si.com/soccer/2020/12/08/lionel-messi-cristiano-ronaldo-barcelona-juventus-champions-league-video), [theScore](https://thescore.com/chlg/news/2066840), [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/match/_/gameId/585770).
- **Correction to the viral post the idea came from:** the return game was **6 weeks later**, not "one week later", and Ronaldo's brace was **two penalties**.
