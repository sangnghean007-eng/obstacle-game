# Learning from big football-cartoon channels (started Nov 16, 2026)
User request: "look at the big channels and learn from them". Learn the *ideas and structure*, never copy their art, characters' designs or jokes.

## Sources checked so far
- **B/R Football "The Champions"** (Bleacher Report; written/directed by the Malamut Brothers, created by Andy Haynes): a spoof *reality show* where all Champions League players live in one chateau. Spin-offs: "Champions Chat" (daytime talk-show parody) and quick skits. B/R says animation gets **+72% cross-platform engagement per post** vs their other football content (self-reported). Tone: pokes fun but is *celebratory, not mocking*. Instagram is by far their biggest platform. ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/bleacher-report-pushes-football-ambitions-new-series-champions/), [Digiday WC](https://digiday.com/media/bleacher-report-launches-youtube-channel-for-its-sports-cartoon-fanbase-ahead-of-world-cup/), [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/bleacher-report-the-champions))
- **442oons**: long-running comedy football animations + parody songs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/442oons)).
- **Goal Makers** (YouTube Shorts): funny football animations, World Cup stories, legends, dramatic matches ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/@GoalMakersFC/shorts)).
- **bodyanimation0** (Instagram, the user's example, studied Nov 16 via the public embed + cover images): **2M followers, 5,299 posts.** Reel "All Highlights of Spain vs Argentina 1-0 Final World Cup 2026 😂🤣" = **1.35M likes, 9.7K comments**. Original audio.
  - **Style:** flat 2D cartoon, thin outlines, almost no shading, plain green pitch / simple set. Full-body characters with long thin legs and huge white boots.
  - **Likeness by EXTREME caricature, not realism:** each player = 1–2 traits blown up to absurd size (a gigantic nose + beard, a huge black hair cloud). Instantly readable and funny at phone size.
  - **The acting carries the joke:** face-to-face stares, hand-on-chin poses, whole teams queuing into a house. Covers have NO text: the image alone is the hook.
  - **Format = speed + volume:** "All highlights of <big match> 😂" right after every big game; a comedic retelling of the real match moments.

## Takeaways to apply
1. **A recurring "world" with recurring characters** (house, locker room, group chat, talk show). Viewers come back for the characters, not only the news. → Our candidates: the World Cup trophy mascot (Ep12–13), the goat judge (Ep10), the VIP box (Pelé/R9), the Oyarzabal ghost 👻.
2. **Signature traits as running gags** (each star has 1–2 exaggerated habits that come back every episode: Ronaldo's SIUUU, Messi's mate, Kane's slip → "NO SLIP", Yamal's sunglasses).
3. **Short skit formats with a familiar frame** (reality show confessional, talk show, group chat, press conference, movie trailer). The frame tells the viewer instantly what kind of joke is coming.
4. **Celebrate while teasing** (rivalry, not hate) — matches our sensitivity rule.
5. **Speed:** react to the news within 24 h with a short skit; a longer "episode" can follow.
6. **Physical comedy + reaction faces** in every beat (see ENGAGEMENT.md §12).

## bodyanimation0 lessons → our actions
1. **Caricature mode:** push our portraits much further (2× the defining trait: Kane's forehead/quiff, Yamal's blond mop, Oyarzabal's long nose, Messi's beard/nose, Ronaldo's jaw). Recognition through exaggeration, not detail.
2. **Simpler sets, more acting:** fewer UI pills on screen; let poses, faces and physical gags tell the story. Covers can be a single funny image, no text.
3. **A fast "ALL HIGHLIGHTS of X vs Y 😂" format** for every big match (within 24 h): retell each real moment as a gag in 30–45 s.
4. **Volume matters:** they post very often; our pipeline should allow a quick 1-day episode alongside bigger ones.

## Study (Nov 19 2026, link from the owner): bodyanimation0 reel Df5L9_2j_RZ

The punchline is an extreme facial distortion (a huge red grinning mouth stretched across the face, bulging eyes, awkward "fake smile" pose) rather than a long story. Low camera angle beside the post with strong perspective on the goal and pitch lines, so even a still frame feels cinematic. Meme framing "<player> this season" = instantly relatable for fans who follow the league.
- Apply: (1) a short format "<player> this season" (12–16 s, one gag, no chapters needed); (2) extreme face morphs for punchlines (giant mouth, eyes popping out, jaw on the floor) instead of only portrait expressions; (3) low/diagonal camera angles with perspective goals and lines. Never copy their art, characters or jokes.
