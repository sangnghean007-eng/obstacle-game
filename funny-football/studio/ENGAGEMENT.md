# Engagement engine: make people WATCH, SHARE and ARGUE
User request (Nov 15 2026): "make videos people find attractive, want to watch, want to share, want to argue about".
Run this **before writing every script**. A script must score at least 14/20 on the scorecard (section 4) to go into production.

## 1. WATCH: keep them until the end
- **Open loop in the first 3 s:** pose a question the video only answers at the end ("WHO WINS?", "ONLY 15 TO GO?!", "NO RONALDO, NO PROBLEM?"). The loop must be closed late, ideally in the last 20%.
- **Layered hook at frame 0:** a big recognisable face (portrait), the hook text and a loud sound all at once.
- **A pattern interrupt every 3–5 s:** a new round or beat, a zoom, a cut-in, a sound change or a slam. Never 5 s with nothing new (a 2-second rule is not supported by the research; 3–5 s is).
- **Escalation:** each beat is bigger than the last (Ep9: shrug → panic → fight → white flag).
- **Payoff + loop:** the twist at the end recontextualises the opening, and the last frame cuts cleanly back to the first. 20–35 s for a viral push.

## 2. SHARE: give them a reason to send it (STEPPS, Jonah Berger)
| Lever | What it means for us | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Social currency** | sharing makes the sharer look funny or "in the know" | a perfectly timed roast of a fresh result; an insider stat few people know |
| **Triggers** | tie the video to something that recurs, so it comes back to mind | "every time Ronaldo scores, the counter moves" (Road to 1000 series); every Clásico |
| **Emotion (high arousal)** | laughter, awe, playful outrage, surprise. Not sadness or calm. | the 1-4 humiliation, the SIUUU shockwave |
| **Public** | a visible signature people copy | the hidden 🐐 hunt, the goat mascot, Team 7 / Team 10 |
| **Practical value** | ammo for real arguments | the clean stat table in the caption, "save this for your next GOAT debate" |
| **Stories** | the facts ride inside a mini-story | how it started → how it's going; a fight with rounds |

The **share trigger line** goes in the caption: "Send this to a Messi fan", "Tag the friend who said Portugal don't need him" (NOT on Facebook, where it counts as bait).

## 3. ARGUE: make them want to comment (identity research)
- **Identity on the line:** people comment most when a result confirms their side or lets them mock the rival. Every episode needs **two camps** (Team 7 vs 10, Portugal fans vs Ronaldo fans, Barça vs Madrid, old GOATs vs new kids).
- **Fair but unfinished:** present the true facts so that **both sides can claim victory** (Ep7/Ep10 3-3), or leave the verdict open ("You decide"). Never call the winner ourselves.
- **Spicy but true:** frame one real stat provocatively ("146 vs 125, and it's not close"). Never invent or distort a fact.
- **"You forgot…" bait (honest version):** say on screen "We left one big stat out 👀 Which one?". Fans will flood in with their favourite stat.
- **Ranking and number questions:** "Which round was the knockout? 1–6", "Rate the SIUUUs 1–4". A number is the easiest comment to leave.
- **Pin a counter-take:** the pinned comment takes the *other* side from the caption to start a thread. Reply in the first hour to keep both camps arguing.
- **Keep it playful:** no insults to people or nations, no family, injuries or tragedies, no derogatory labels. Rivalry, not hate. Hold "potentially inappropriate" comments on YouTube.

## 6. Anti-monotony rule (user, Nov 15 2026: "isn't it too monotonous?")
Repeating beats every 3–5 s is not enough: **the beats themselves must differ.** In any series of similar segments (rounds, goals, exhibits, attempts):
- **Rotate the stage:** location, palette and lighting change each segment.
- **Rotate the gimmick:** combo counter, anime special-move strip, slow-mo, number counter, camera whip/reveal, full-screen cut-in, split screen, freeze frame.
- **Rotate the reaction layout:** both faces, loser only, winner only, full-screen, none.
- **Vary the lengths:** mix quick and longer segments (e.g. 3.4–4.6 s) and put the biggest one last.
- **Escalate:** the final segment is the biggest spectacle.
Add a line to the scorecard: **11. Do no two consecutive segments share a stage, gimmick AND reaction layout? (0–2)**. The total becomes /22; ship at 15+.

## 7. Format-level variety (user, Nov 15 2026: "still monotonous, be more creative")
Rotating backgrounds and gimmicks inside ONE format is still monotonous; the viewer sees the same template. For list-type content (comparisons, records, attempts), **change the genre per segment**: fighting game, kart race, platform jump, space shooter, pinball, quiz show, boss fight, cooking show, nature documentary, news bulletin, horror trailer…
- **Each genre brings its own camera, UI, physics, palette and sound world**, so every segment feels like a new video.
- **Keep one thread** (a running score pill, the same two characters, the mascot) so the story still connects.
- Ask before scripting: **"Could a viewer predict the next segment's look?"** If yes, change the genre.

## 8. Drama toolkit (learned on Ep11, Nov 15 2026)
- **A live race counter with lead changes and ties** (e.g. a Ballon d'Or race 1-1 → 4-1 → 5-5 😱) creates real suspense even in a timeline. Tease the tie in the hook ("wait for 2017 😱").
- **Build-ups before the biggest beats:** heartbeat, drumroll, blackout, then an explosion of rays and confetti.
- **The rival always reacts:** jealousy escalation (🙄 → 😤 → steam), a deadpan signature prop (Messi's mate), both staring at each other across the split.
- **Physical collision gags** when both lives cross (suitcases BONK at the divider).
- **End on an open loop:** a "TO BE CONTINUED ➡️" freeze makes people follow for part 2.

## 9. Creative-first, proactive (user, Nov 15 2026: "think creatively, why so simple? don't wait for me to guide you")
A direct remake of a meme/list is NOT a video. Before scripting:
- **Write 3+ bold concepts** in different frames (personify an object, a fake genre: dating show/documentary/heist, a "what if", a POV of a non-human narrator) and pick the boldest one that still keeps the facts true.
- **Self-critique as a viewer:** "Is this just the meme in motion?" If yes, throw it away.
- **Give the story a narrator or a want** (Ep12: the trophy remembers its "exes"; the CR7 beat becomes a flirt, not an insult).
- **Every era/segment gets its own look AND sound world** (film crackle 1958, VHS 1994, samba 2002, fireworks + choir 2022, smooth jazz for the CR7 "date").
- **Hunt for the little-known true detail** that makes the joke (R9 played 0 minutes in 1994; the Cascão haircut; the bisht).
- **Decide and ship.** Don't ask the user to choose between bland options; show the bold version, keep the safe one as a backup in `review/`.

## 10. Kill the "standing template" (user, Nov 15 2026: "you keep making the same old mistake: monotonous and bland")
Ep12 v1 and v2 both failed for the same root cause, even though the backgrounds changed: **every beat = one character standing in the middle + a prop appears + a caption pill**. The viewer sees the same shot 5 times, and the joke lives in the text, not in the action.
Before rendering, check the contact sheet. If 3+ frames have the same composition (one figure centered, standing, caption above), rebuild.
What works instead (Ep12 v3):
- **Continuous action:** one camera move or one chase that carries through the whole video (side-scroller, race, escape, heist). Something is always running, falling, flying or crashing.
- **Rivals on screen TOGETHER** and physically interacting (bonk, both fall in holes, one benched while the other dives).
- **Slapstick > captions:** each fact becomes a physical gag (out on penalties = a giant glove swats him; group-stage exit = he clangs into a barrier; the same-day exit = two holes open at once). The caption only confirms it.
- **Running threads + callbacks:** a live scoreboard, a reaction box (Pelé/R9 in VIP), and a callback (the Spain runners in 2010 come back in 2026).
- **Real-but-surprising facts** are the best gags: dig for coincidences (same day, same rival twice, walked past the trophy).
