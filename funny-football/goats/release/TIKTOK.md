## B. Steps in the app (+ → Upload)
1. **Select the video** → Next. Keep the **original sound**. Do not add a trending track over it; the sound effects carry the jokes. If you want a trending sound for reach, add it at very low volume [VERIFY: only tracks from TikTok's licensed library].
2. **Cover**: choose the frame at about 0.4 s (both stamped RETIRED), or upload `cover-9x16.jpg` if your app offers "Upload" [VERIFY]. Add cover text only if the frame has none (it already does).
3. **Caption** (max 2,200 characters [REFERENCE]; the feed shows about the first 100 [REFERENCE]). Put the searched keywords first; TikTok uses caption words for search and topic matching [REFERENCE]:
```
Messi retirement + Ronaldo retires = same year 😭 Ronaldo paid his €255 fine like a parking ticket 💸 Team 10 or Team 7? (parody)
#messi #ronaldo #ronaldoretires #football #worldcup2026
```
   **Hashtags**: 3–5, at the end; mix broad (#football) with specific (#ronaldoretires) [REFERENCE].
4. **Mentions**: do not @mention the players.
5. **Location**: none.
6. **Who can watch**: Everyone. **Allow comments**: on. **Allow Duet / Stitch**: on (helps spread). **Allow downloads**: your call.
7. **More options → AI-generated content**: **off**. TikTok requires the label for AI-made or AI-edited content showing **realistic** people or scenes; this is an original 2D cartoon [REFERENCE: AP/Fortune coverage of TikTok's policy; VERIFY on support.tiktok.com]. Turn it on if you ever use realistic likeness or voices.
8. **Content disclosure / branded content**: off (no sponsor).
9. **Upload quality**: turn on high-quality upload if the option exists [VERIFY].
10. **Post**: now, while the Nov 5 news is fresh, or schedule (TikTok Studio on desktop) for the peak hours in *Analytics → Followers* [VERIFY].
