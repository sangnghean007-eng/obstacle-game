## B. How to post
**Recommended**: **Meta Business Suite** on desktop (Create reel), so you can schedule, upload the cover from a file and read Insights in one place. Alternative: Facebook app → Create → Reel.

1. **Upload the video** and keep the **original audio** (no library music; the sounds are part of the jokes).
2. **Caption** (max about 2,200 characters [REFERENCE]; the feed shows about 125–140 characters [REFERENCE]; one analysis found 30–60-word captions get the best engagement [REFERENCE, single source]):
```
Messi & Ronaldo both retired from their national teams in 2026 — same year! 😳
Messi pressed RETIRE for the 2nd time (2016 says hi 👋). Ronaldo paid his €255 fine like a parking ticket 💸
Team 10 or Team 7: who comes back first? 👇
Real facts · parody cartoon · both still play club football.
#Messi #Ronaldo #FootballMemes #WorldCup2026
```
   **Hashtags**: 3–4, **in the caption**, not in comments [REFERENCE]. No hashtag stuffing; several sources say Meta reduces reach for stuffed or unrelated captions [REFERENCE].
3. **Thumbnail**: upload `cover-9x16.jpg` (Business Suite: Thumbnail → Upload) [VERIFY].
4. **Audience**: **Public**.
5. **Tag / Collaborator**: do not tag the players. If partnering with a football fan Page, invite it as a collaborator [VERIFY availability].
6. **AI label**: **off**. Meta's Help page requires labels only for photorealistic video or realistic audio that was digitally created or altered; cartoon-style video is given as an example that does not need one [OFFICIAL, VERIFY].
7. **Other options** [VERIFY names]:
   - **Allow remixing**: on.
   - **Comments**: on; set Page moderation filters (Page → Settings → Moderation) to block insults.
8. **Timing**: publish now while the news is fresh, or schedule in Business Suite for the hours *Insights → Audience* shows your followers are most active.

## D. Risks
- **Fan wars / insults to the players**: moderation filters, hide comments.
- **Misinformation reports**: the caption states parody and the real situation (both still play club football).
- **Engagement bait** ("like if…"): don't use it; use the binary question instead [REFERENCE].
